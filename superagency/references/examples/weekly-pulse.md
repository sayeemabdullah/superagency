# Example — Weekly Pulse

> **Illustrative only.** Every company, number, and quote below is invented for shape. Never reuse these figures, and never present them as evidence.

---

**Week of 10-16 August**

**Wins**
- Trial starts 412, up 18% on last week — highest week this quarter
- Organic sessions to /pricing 3,100, up 9% after the title tag fix shipped Monday
- LinkedIn post on close cycles drove 240 sessions, 6x our post average

**Misses**
- Demo requests 22, down 9%. Third consecutive weekly decline
- Paid search CPA $118 against a $90 target
- Trial-to-activation 31%, flat for four weeks

**Anomalies**
Tuesday's signup spike (89 in one day) traced to a partner newsletter we didn't
know had shipped. Not repeatable — exclude it before reading the weekly trend.

**Careful with this one**
Impressions are up 40% while conversions are flat. That reads as a win in the
dashboard and isn't one — we're buying broader, cheaper traffic that doesn't
convert.

**Last week's action**
"Fix /pricing title tags." Shipped Monday. Organic sessions up 9% — plausibly
related, but a competitor also depublished a comparison page the same week, so
don't bank the whole gain on our change.

**This week's one action**
Cut the two broad-match paid keywords driving the CPA overrun. Not a landing
page redesign, not a new channel — the CPA problem is upstream of both.
