## Ledgerly — messaging framework

### Positioning statement
For **finance leads at 20-200 person companies** who **close the books in 5-8 days on spreadsheets**, **Ledgerly** is a **month-end close platform** that **cuts the close to one day by automating reconciliation**. Unlike **general accounting suites**, we **do only the close, and do it without a services engagement**.

### Messaging hierarchy

**Core message**
The close is slow because reconciliation is manual — not because the team is small.

| Pillar | Proof points |
|---|---|
| Automates the work, not just the tracking | Rules-based matching across bank, AP, and AR feeds; one named customer cut the close from 6 days to 1 |
| Live for the team in days, not a quarter | Self-serve setup, no implementation fee, median time-to-first-close under two weeks |
| Auditor-ready by default | Every adjustment carries a trail; the export pack is built from the ledger, not rebuilt by hand |

Anything that doesn't ladder to one of these three pillars doesn't go in the copy.

### Value prop, three ways
- **Benefit:** close in a day, every month.
- **Specific:** cut the month-end close from 6 days to 1 without adding headcount.
- **Differentiated:** the only close tool that reconciles for you and needs no implementation project.

### What this framework refuses to claim
- No time-savings figure beyond the one named customer. Everything else is "early customers report faster closes," labelled as such.
- "Auditor-ready" is a design claim, not a certification. Legal cleared the phrasing; it is not a SOC report or a regulatory statement.
