**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## Ledgerly raises $12M Series A to cut the month-end close to a single day

**San Francisco — 4 March 2026** — Ledgerly, the reconciliation platform for
finance teams, today announced a $12M Series A led by Corner Ventures, with
participation from existing investors. The company will use the funding to
expand its data-connector library and grow its engineering team.

Mid-market finance teams still close their books in five to eight days, most of
it spent matching transactions by hand across banking, AP, and AR systems.
Ledgerly automates that matching and produces an auditor-ready trail as a
by-product, so the close becomes a review step rather than a rebuild.

"We started Ledgerly because every controller we talked to had the same first
week of the month — long, manual, and impossible to speed up by hiring," said
Dana Whitfield, CEO and co-founder of Ledgerly. *(Draft quote — for Dana to
approve or rewrite before this goes out.)*

Since launching in 2025, Ledgerly reports that early customers have shortened
their close by an average of several days; specific figures vary by team and
are not being published. The company now serves finance teams at companies
between 20 and 200 employees.

"The close is one of the last finance workflows still done mostly in
spreadsheets," said a partner at Corner Ventures. *(Draft quote — investor to
approve wording.)*

### About Ledgerly
Ledgerly is a reconciliation and close-management platform for mid-market
finance teams. It is based in San Francisco. More at ledgerly.example.
