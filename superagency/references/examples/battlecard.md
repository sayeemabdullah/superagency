## Ledgerly vs Reconcile.io

**Their positioning (their words, paraphrased)**
An all-in-one finance platform — close, spend management, and forecasting.

**Built for**
250+ employee companies with a finance team of five or more.

**Pricing**
Public: from $1,400/month, annual only. Implementation fee quoted separately.

**Their pitch → our counter → proof**
| They say | We say | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| "One platform for all of finance" | Breadth costs you a 6-week implementation before anything works | Their own docs quote a 4-6 week onboarding |
| "Enterprise-grade controls" | True, and priced for enterprise. Below 200 people you pay for governance you don't use | Their pricing page starts at $1,400/mo |
| "Trusted by 400 finance teams" | Fair — they are established. We're faster to value for smaller teams | *[supply our own onboarding time here]* |

**Where they're genuinely strong** — multi-entity consolidation. If the prospect
has three or more legal entities, we lose on capability. Say so and move on.

**Where they're vulnerable** — time to first value, and annual-only contracts
that make a trial impossible.

**White space** — nobody in this category is selling to the 20-50 person company
that has exactly one finance hire. Both vendors above start above that line.

**Do not say** — anything about their reliability or security posture. We have
no evidence, and unsubstantiated comparative claims are a legal risk.
