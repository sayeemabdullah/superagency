> **Illustrative only.** Every company, number, and quote below is invented for shape. Never reuse these figures, and never present them as evidence.

## Ledgerly — "Close in a Day" launch

**Objective & primary metric**
1,200 trial starts by 30 November. Not "raise awareness."

**Audience**
Finance leads at 20-200 person companies who currently close the books in 5-8
days using spreadsheets plus their accounting tool. They believe a faster close
requires more headcount.

**Core message**
The month-end close is slow because reconciliation is manual, not because the
team is small.

**Channels**
| Channel | Why this one |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn organic | Audience is demonstrably there; our last 6 posts on close cycles outperformed everything else |
| Paid search | Existing intent on "month end close software"; we already rank page 2 organically |
| Partner newsletter | Reaches controllers we cannot target by title on LinkedIn |

Podcast sponsorship was cut — no way to attribute it inside the window.

**Timeline**
| Week | Milestone | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| -3 | Landing page copy final | Positioning sign-off |
| -2 | Design assets delivered | Copy final |
| -1 | Paid campaigns built, not live | Assets |
| 0 | Launch across all channels same day | Legal review of the "one day" claim |
| +1 | First customer proof post | A customer agreeing to be named |
| +3 | Case study published | Results data from that customer |

**Budget**
$18,000 media, $4,000 production. Split 70/20/10 across paid search, LinkedIn,
and an untested channel.

**Risks & open questions**
- "Close in a day" is a performance claim and needs legal sign-off. If it doesn't
  clear, the whole campaign name changes — decide by week -3, not week 0.
- No named customer has agreed to the +1 proof post yet. Without one, that slot
  is empty and the post-launch phase weakens.
- Trial-to-paid rate is unknown for this segment, so trial starts is the honest
  metric here. Revenue impact is not forecastable yet — say so rather than
  modelling it.
